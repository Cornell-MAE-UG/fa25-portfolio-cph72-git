## About Me


![Profile Picture]({{ "assets/images/me/profile_photo_2026.JPG" | relative_url }}){: class="profile-image"}

 
My name is Connor Hyde and I am currently a third-year student enrolled at Cornell University majoring in Mechanical Engineering. On campus I am involved in many student organizations. A project team I am involved in is Cornell Hyperloop, where we develop a prototype hyperloop. I am the Track Team Lead and Machining Lead, so I am currently conducting research on track geometry and materials that will best allow my team and me to achieve levitation in the near future. Additionally, I am a staff member of the Manufacturing Learning Studio, where I teach students all the basics on using a manual Lathe and Mill, certifying them as red apron machinists. I am also the house manager of Sigma Pi Fraternity, and a member of Cornell Rock Climbing Club and Cornell Club Lacrosse.
After graduation, I plan to enter the field of aerospace or robotics, looking for a career where I can work on projects from start to finish and make key decisions throughout the project's full lifecycle. I have found this kind of work to be the most rewarding, taking a complex problem, planning a solution, and building something real. Longer term, I hope to grow into roles where I have even broader ownership over systems and teams, whether that is as a lead engineer or in a technical management capacity. Both robotics and aerospace are fields where I believe that kind of growth is possible, and where the work itself will continue to be challenging and meaningful.
